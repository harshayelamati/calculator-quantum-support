Calculator Quantum privacy policy

Effective October 25, 2026

Calculator Quantum is a scientific calculator, physics formula solver and unit converter. It is designed to work entirely on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party services, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your device

Your recent calculation history (up to 50 items) and the formulas you mark as favorites are saved in the app's private storage on your device so they are still there next time you open the app. They are never uploaded, shared or read by anyone else. You can clear the history with the Clear history button, and deleting the app removes everything. Sketches you draw on iPad stay on the device and are never uploaded.

Clipboard

If you tap Copy on a history item, that one result is placed on your clipboard so you can paste it elsewhere. The app never reads your clipboard.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or any other personal information.

Children

Because the app collects no data, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, the updated policy will be posted at this address with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue at https://github.com/harshayelamati/calculator-quantum-support/issues.
