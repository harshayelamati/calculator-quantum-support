Calculator Quantum support

Help for the scientific calculator, formula solver and unit converter.

Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/harshayelamati/calculator-quantum-support/issues with your device model, iOS version and what you were doing. Please include a screenshot if you can.

Common questions

How do I solve for a variable in Physics mode?

Pick a category and formula, fill in every field except the one you want, and tap Solve. Leave exactly one field empty.

Why did Solve show an error?

Either more or fewer than one field was empty, or the inputs have no valid answer, such as dividing by zero.

What is the difference between Common and Extended in Convert?

Common uses Apple's built-in unit system for everyday quantities. Extended covers the full NIST reference table, including CGS and traditional engineering units such as dynes, poise and slugs.

How do I dismiss the keyboard?

Tap any empty area of the screen.

Why is Sketch missing on my iPhone?

Sketch is an Apple Pencil feature and is available on iPad only.

Does the app store my data?

No. Everything stays on your device. See the privacy policy.
